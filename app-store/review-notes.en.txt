BabyKlar is a Norwegian-language planning app for expectant and new parents. It helps users keep an inventory of baby equipment, prioritise preparation tasks, track clothing by size, pack a hospital bag, and share a gift wish list. It is free, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. No account, sign-in, or invitation is required.

NEW IN 1.1.0: SHAREABLE WISH LIST
1. Complete onboarding (any answers; the baby's name is optional).
2. Open "Ønsker" (Wishes, the gift icon in the tab bar). Tap "Legg til ønske", type any item, for example "Bæresele", and tap "Legg til".
3. Tap "Lag lenke" (Create link). The wishes are uploaded to our server at babyklar.app and the iOS share sheet opens with the link. You can close the share sheet.
4. Tap "Åpne" (Open) to view the link in an in-app browser. This is the page family and friends see, with no app or account needed. Tap "Reserver" (Reserve) on a wish.
5. Close the browser and open another tab, then "Ønsker" again. The app now shows "1 gave er reservert" (1 gift reserved). The app is told that a wish is reserved, never by whom.
6. "Slutt å dele" (Stop sharing) deletes the list from the server. The link then shows "Fant ikke ønskelisten" (wish list not found).

Only the wishes (name, size, note, priority, and whether used items are fine) are uploaded, and only after the user taps "Lag lenke". The baby's name, due date and the rest of the app stay on the device. Guests are anonymous: reserving stores only a random browser cookie so the same browser can undo it. Shared lists the app has not contacted for 12 months are deleted automatically.

PHOTO RECOGNITION (unchanged from 1.0.0)
The camera button in the middle of the tab bar lets the user take or choose a photo of baby items. The selected photo, and the names of already registered non-clothing items, are sent through our server to OpenAI to suggest items. The user reviews the suggestions before anything is saved. The feature is optional and needs internet access; items can always be added manually. Camera and photo access use the standard iOS permission prompts.

OTHER NOTES
Everything else is stored locally on the device. "Baby" settings (the icon at the top of Home) contain backup export and restore, and "Slett alle lokale data", which resets the app; test it last. A hidden demo switch (tap the version line at the bottom of Baby settings five times) fills the app with example data for demonstrations. It is not needed for review.

The app is intended for adults preparing for a baby, not for use by children. It is a practical planning tool, not a medical service.
