BabyKlar is a Norwegian-language planning app for expectant and new parents. It helps users keep an inventory of baby equipment, prioritise preparation tasks, track clothing by size, and pack a hospital bag. It is free, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. No account, sign-in, or invitation is required.

Suggested review path:
1. Launch the app and complete onboarding. Select "Første barn" (first child), a future due date, and any household preferences. The baby's name is optional.
2. On "Hjem" (Home), inspect the preparation summary and prioritised tasks.
3. Open "Ting" (Items). Open an item and change its status to "Har" (Have), "Mangler" (Missing), or "Trenger ikke" (Not needed). Return to Home to see the updated summary.
4. Open "Plan" and complete a task.
5. From Home, open "Garderobe" (Wardrobe) and select a clothing size.
6. From Home, open "Sykehusbag" (Hospital bag), then "Start pakkemodus" (Start packing mode). Mark an item as packed.
7. Open the bear icon at the top of Home to access "Baby" settings. "Avansert" (Advanced) contains backup export and restore. "Slett alle lokale data" deletes/resets the app's local user data. Test deletion last.

The inventory and preparation state are stored locally on the device. An exported backup is shared only using the destination chosen by the user. There is no automatic household synchronisation.
